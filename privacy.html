Privacy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

TaxXML.ca is a free tool from AVANZA Business Automation. It turns a spreadsheet into XML for certain Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) information returns. This notice describes what the site actually does with information. It is written for people using the site in Canada.

Spreadsheet and slip data stay in your browser

Choosing an NR4, T4, T1204, or T5018 file does not upload that file to a TaxXML server. There is no TaxXML account and no TaxXML database of slips.

  • The file is read in your browser and parsed there. Names, addresses, amounts, social insurance numbers (SIN), and business numbers (BN) stay in that browser tab while you work.
  • The XML file is built in the browser and downloaded to your computer. TaxXML does not send it to the CRA. You submit it yourself if you use Internet File Transfer (IFT).
  • Closing the tab clears the spreadsheet and the generated XML from memory. That does not clear the filing details described below.

After the page and its scripts have loaded, the conversion itself does not need a network connection. The first visit does, because the page, fonts, and libraries are downloaded then.

Filing details saved on this device

Step 3 filing details are saved in this browser’s localStorage under the key taxxml_filing_details, so you do not have to retype them on a later visit. The site writes them when you change a saved field or leave it. They are not sent to AVANZA.

What is saved:

  • Transmitter CRA account number, RepID (Represent a Client), transmitter name, submission reference ID, language, contact name, phone, and email
  • Payer or employer account number, NR4 non-resident account number, organization name, address, city, province, postal code, contact name, and phone
  • T4 dental benefit code (Box 45), when that field is on the form

When those details are restored, the last character of the submission reference ID is advanced (for example, A becomes B) so a later download is less likely to reuse the same reference.

The Try it with sample data button fills empty filing fields with made-up demo values. Those demo values are not saved, and they are cleared when you open your own file.

If you answer or close the “What brings you here?” question, the site stores the flag taxxml_intent_asked in this browser’s sessionStorage so it does not ask again until you close the tab. Your answer itself is not stored.

What is not saved in localStorage: the spreadsheet, the generated XML, the taxation year, the report type (original, amendment, or cancel), and the T5018 fiscal-period end date.

You can remove the saved details with the Clear link on the filing step, or by clearing site data for taxxml.ca in your browser. Anyone who uses the same browser profile can see the saved fields.

What leaves the browser

Your slips, SIN, BN, and Step 3 filing details are not included in the requests below. Other traffic does leave the browser.

GitHub Pages. The site is static files hosted on GitHub Pages. Loading a page, stylesheet, image, or sample CSV template means GitHub receives an ordinary request for that file, including your IP address and browser. Those requests do not contain your spreadsheet.

Google Analytics 4. Pages load Google’s tag from www.googletagmanager.com with measurement ID G-E2F5Q3RZVH. Local copies of the site (localhost, 127.0.0.1, or a file opened from disk) don’t load it. Besides pageviews, the site sends a few usage events: return_selected, file_opened, file_parse_error, step_reached, picker_opened, generate_clicked, validation_failed, validation_passed, xml_downloaded, start_fresh, cta_click, sample_downloaded, sample_loaded, intent_selected, column_remapped, cell_edited, table_shown, table_error_state, table_exited, row_deleted, table_menu_opened, table_help_opened, table_hscroll, province_mapped, and app_error. They carry only these values:

  • the return type (return_type);
  • how the return was chosen (source: default when a file is opened with the preselected return, landing when a guide page’s link chose it, hero for the buttons at the top of the home page, or dropdown for the Step 1 list);
  • the step number and whether you moved forward, back, or started over (step, direction);
  • an error count and a general error category (error_count, error_category);
  • for validation_failed, the name of the first field that failed (first_error_field). This is one of the converter’s own column or form-field names, such as sin or tx-email. Anything else, including your own column headers, is sent as other;
  • for file_opened: whether the file is one of the site’s own sample templates, either opened with “Try it with sample data” or downloaded and opened again, worked out on your device (is_sample); a row-count range such as 6-20 (rows_bucket); and how many required columns weren’t matched automatically (required_unmapped);
  • for cta_click, a short page name such as home, t4 or cra-xml-samples (page) and which button or link was clicked, such as nav, hero or sample_try (cta);
  • for sample_loaded, where the sample data was asked for (page: home for the Step 1 button, or landing for a link on another page of the site);
  • for column_remapped (you changed which CRA field a column of the Step 2 table feeds) and cell_edited (you changed a cell in that table), the converter’s own name for the field, such as sin, full_name or ignore (field), and for column_remapped whether drag-and-drop, the ← / → buttons or another button (Leave out, Use column, Use as full name) was used (method: drag, keyboard or button; before November 2026 a dropdown sent dropdown). Never the cell’s value or your column header;
  • for table_shown (the Step 2 table appears), table_error_state (Continue or Generate is stopped by the table) and table_exited (you leave the table): the return type, is_sample, rows_bucket and required_unmapped as above; a column-count range such as 9-16 (cols_bucket); how many cells are marked red or amber (error_cells, warning_cells) and a range for the red ones such as 3-10 (error_cells_bucket); and which of the converter’s own column names have red cells, alone and with a count, such as sin and sin:2 (first_error_field, error_field_list, error_by_field);
  • for table_error_state, whether Continue or Generate was stopped (trigger: continue or generate);
  • for table_exited, how you left the table (exit_reason: continue, back, reset or pagehide when the page was closed, then sent with transport_type beacon); the seconds spent on it and a range such as 11-30 (step2_seconds, step2_seconds_bucket); and how many columns you moved, cells you changed and rows you deleted while there (remaps_count, edits_count, deletes_count);
  • for row_deleted (you deleted a row of the table), the row-count range after the delete (rows_bucket); for table_menu_opened (you opened a column’s “⋯” menu), only the return type; for table_hscroll (you scrolled the table sideways), cols_bucket;
  • for province_mapped (we turned province names in your file into CRA codes, such as British Columbia to BC), how many cells changed (count), never the names or codes themselves;
  • for table_help_opened, which help you opened (help_kind: keyboard for “Keyboard & help”, or cell_tip for the note on a red or amber cell, with the converter’s own name for that column in field). Never the note’s text;
  • for app_error (the site itself hit an error, described under “Feedback and error reports” below), the kind of error (error_kind: js_error, xml_generate_failed or parse_crash) and the return type when one is chosen. Never the error message or stack;
  • for intent_selected, the answer to the optional “What brings you here?” question (intent: filing_now, exploring_format, or for_client).

The events never include spreadsheet cells, the values you type into filing fields, your column headers, file names, SINs, names, or amounts. The Step 2 table events send only counts, ranges and the converter’s own column names, never a cell, a header, or the text of an error or warning. Opening the site with ?internal=1 is meant for the site owner’s own testing. It saves the key taxxml_internal in this browser’s localStorage (?internal=0 removes it), and the browser’s hits then carry traffic_type set to internal so they can be left out of reports. ?debug_mode=1 adds debug_mode to that page’s hits for Google’s debugging view. Google can receive the page address, rough location, device and browser details, and a client identifier stored in cookies (such as _ga). Google’s policy is at policies.google.com/privacy.

Feedback and error reports (Cloudflare Worker). The Feedback link at the top and bottom of each page opens a Feedback form for a comment, a question, or a bug report. Separately, if the site itself breaks while you use it (a script error, a crash while reading your file, or a failure while building the XML), your browser sends an automatic error report. Both go to a small Cloudflare Worker run by AVANZA. That request is processed on Cloudflare’s network, which includes servers in the United States. The Worker passes each report on to AVANZA as a Slack message and, when it is set up, as an email sent through Resend. Until the Worker is set up, the Feedback link simply opens avanza-automation.com, and no feedback and error reports are sent from the site.

  • Only the message and optional email you type in the Feedback form leave the browser, plus a scrubbed technical report (return type, step, browser, error kind, scrubbed stack) and the site version. The error kind is js_error, xml_generate_failed or parse_crash. The scrubbed stack lists only the site’s own script files and line numbers. Numbers, quoted values, capitalised words such as names, file names, email addresses and web addresses are removed from the error text in your browser, and again by the Worker.
  • Feedback and error reports never include your slip data, spreadsheet cells, SINs, BNs, names, amounts, Step 3 filing details, or file names. The form asks you not to paste them, and SIN- or BN-like numbers are removed from your message. Anything else you type is sent as written.
  • The form is protected by Cloudflare Turnstile, which checks that a person, not a bot, is sending it. Turnstile loads from challenges.cloudflare.com only when you open the Feedback form. Cloudflare receives your IP address and browser details for that check. Cloudflare’s policy is at cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.
  • Reports are not stored in a TaxXML database. The site stays static files on GitHub Pages, and the Worker forwards each report and does not keep a copy. AVANZA keeps the Slack message or email it receives. Cloudflare, Slack and Resend keep their own logs under their own policies.
  • Ordinary problems you can fix yourself, such as a wrong file type or a field that fails validation, are not reported this way. Local copies of the site (localhost, 127.0.0.1, or a file opened from disk) never send reports.

Google Fonts. Pages request the IBM Plex Sans and IBM Plex Mono fonts from fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com. Your browser sends that request, including your IP address, to Google.

SheetJS. The converter page loads the SheetJS library from cdn.sheetjs.com (xlsx-0.20.3). That is a script download. The spreadsheet is parsed on your device after the library loads. It is not uploaded to that CDN. The Privacy and Terms pages do not load SheetJS.

Lucide. The converter page loads icons from unpkg.com/lucide. That is a script download from the unpkg CDN. It does not receive your spreadsheet or filing details. The Privacy and Terms pages do not load Lucide.

Stripe. The site does not embed a Stripe script. If you follow “buying me a coffee”, your browser opens Stripe’s donation page at donate.stripe.com. Payment details are entered on Stripe’s site, not on TaxXML. AVANZA does not receive your card number through this site. Stripe’s policy is at stripe.com/privacy.

Links you choose to open. Links to the CRA IFT sign-in page and to AVANZA’s websites go to those sites only if you click them. TaxXML does not pass your XML file along with the click.

Cookies

TaxXML’s own pages do not set cookies. Google Analytics, loaded as described above, uses cookies under Google’s default setup. The filing details in localStorage are stored by the browser, and they are not cookies.

How long information is kept

Slip data in memory lasts until you close the tab or start over. Saved filing details stay in localStorage until you clear them or your browser removes them. TaxXML has no copy of either. Feedback and error reports are kept only as the Slack message or email AVANZA receives. Google, GitHub, Stripe, Cloudflare, Slack, Resend, and the CDNs named above keep their own logs under their own policies.

Children

The tool is for people preparing Canadian information returns. It is not directed at children.

Changes

If the site starts handling information differently, this page will be updated. The date at the top is the latest revision.
