What the tool is

TaxXML.ca converts a spreadsheet in your browser into an XML file for Canadian information returns the tool currently supports: T4 (Statement of Remuneration Paid), T1204 (Government Service Contract Payments), and T5018 (Statement of Contract Payments). You download the file. If you file it, you do that yourself through the CRA Internet File Transfer (IFT) service, or another channel you choose.

The tool is a converter. It does not open a CRA account, transmit a return, or confirm that the CRA has accepted a file.
