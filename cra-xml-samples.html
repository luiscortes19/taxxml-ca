Sample CRA XML files for T4, NR4, T5018 and T1204

Generated by TaxXML from its sample spreadsheets

These are complete XML files of the kind you upload to CRA's Internet File Transfer (IFT), one per return, for the 2027 filing season (tax year 2026). Each one was generated by the TaxXML converter from its sample spreadsheet and validates against CRA XML schema 1-27-1, which CRA accepts from January 11, 2027. Until December 22, 2026, CRA accepts schema 1-26-3 instead. The T4, T5018 and T1204 files here are valid under 1-26-3 too; the NR4 file is not, because 1-26-3 has no CIN element. CRA publishes a new schema for each filing year, so check which version applies when you file. Every name, SIN, account number and amount in them is made up, and the transmitter and payer are marked DEMO. Don't file these files.

Examples, not advice

These samples show the structure of a valid file. They are not tax, legal, or accounting advice. CRA specifications change, so always check your own files against the CRA's official XML specifications for information returns before you file.

The T619 wrapper every file shares

Every file is one <Submission> with a <T619> transmitter record, then a <Return> holding one return: its slips first, then its summary. CRA rejects empty optional tags, so optional fields with no value are left out entirely.

  • TransmitterAccountNumber / bn15: the transmitter's CRA account number, usually the 15-character business number.
  • sbmt_ref_id: Submission Reference ID, up to 8 letters or digits, new for every file you send.
  • summ_cnt: the number of summaries in the file. TaxXML always sends one.
  • lang_cd: language of the submission, E or F.
  • TransmitterName, TransmitterCountryCode: who is sending the file, and their country.
  • CNTC: the transmitter's contact name, area code, phone number and email.

T4 sample XML

Two employees, one employer. Download T4_sample.xml · T4 XML guide · Make it yourself from the sample data

  • EMPE_NM, sin: the employee's name and 9-digit social insurance number.
  • bn: the employer's payroll account, for example 123456789RP0001, on every slip and the summary.
  • cpp_qpp_xmpt_cd, ei_xmpt_cd: CPP/QPP and EI exemption indicators, 0 or 1.
  • empt_prov_cd: province of employment, as a 2-letter code.
  • empr_dntl_ben_rpt_cd: the dental benefits code (Box 45).
  • T4_AMT: the box amounts, such as employment income (Box 14), CPP contributions, EI premiums and income tax deducted.
  • tx_yr, slp_cnt, T4_TAMT: on the summary, the tax year, the number of slips and the totals.
  • rpt_tcd: report type. O for original, A for amendment, C for cancel.

NR4 sample XML

Two non-resident recipients: an individual and a corporation. Download NR4_sample.xml · NR4 XML guide · Make it yourself from the sample data

  • nr_acct_nbr: the payer's non-resident account number, 3 letters and 6 digits. The same number goes on the summary and every slip.
  • RCPNT_NM or ENTPRS_NM: the recipient's name. Individuals and joint accounts use RCPNT_NM; corporations and everyone else use ENTPRS_NM.
  • RCPNT_ADDR: a foreign address, so it uses ste_cd and fgn_pstl_cd instead of a province and postal code.
  • tx_cntry_cd, fssn_nbr: the recipient's 3-letter country of tax residence (Box 12) and their foreign tax identification number (Box 30 on 2027 slips).
  • CIN, new in schema 1-27-1: the recipient's Canadian tax identification number (Box 13), as sin_ttn_itn, bn or trust. Neither sample recipient has one, so both carry <sin_ttn_itn>000000000</sin_ttn_itn>, as CRA instructs.
  • rcpnt_tcd: recipient type. 1 individual, 2 joint account, 3 corporation, 4 other, 5 government or international organization.
  • inc_1_tcd, crcy_1_cd: the income code (Box 14) and currency code (Box 15).
  • NR4_AMT: gross income (Box 16) and non-resident tax withheld (Box 17).
  • rpt_tcd: report type. O for original, A for amendment, C for cancel on a slip. The summary takes O or A only.

T5018 sample XML

Two subcontractors: a corporation and an individual. Download T5018_sample.xml · T5018 XML guide · Make it yourself from the sample data

  • bn: the payer's 15-character account number on the RZ program, for example 123456789RZ0001, on every slip and the summary.
  • RCPNT_NM or CORP_PTNRP_NM: the subcontractor's name. Individuals use RCPNT_NM; corporations and partnerships use CORP_PTNRP_NM.
  • sin, rcpnt_bn: the subcontractor's social insurance number or business number (Box 24). Both tags appear on every slip; the one that doesn't apply gets a zero placeholder.
  • rcpnt_tcd: recipient type, such as individual or corporation.
  • sbctrcr_amt: the construction subcontract payments for the period (Box 22).
  • PRD_END_DT: on the summary, the day, month and year the reporting period ends.
  • slp_cnt, tot_sbctrcr_amt: on the summary, the number of slips and the total paid.
  • rpt_tcd: report type. O for original, A for amendment, C for cancel.

T1204 sample XML

Two contractors: a corporation paid for services only and a sole proprietor paid for mixed goods and services. Download T1204_sample.xml · T1204 XML guide · Make it yourself from the sample data

  • payr_bn: on each slip, the paying department's 15-character payroll (RP) account number. The summary carries it as bn.
  • RCPNT_NM, RCPNT_BUS_NM: the recipient's name (sole proprietors) and business name.
  • sin, rcpnt_bn: the recipient's social insurance number or business number. Both tags appear on every slip; the one that doesn't apply gets a zero placeholder.
  • rcpnt_tcd: recipient type: 1 sole proprietor, 3 corporation, 4 partnership.
  • T1204_AMT: the amount paid, as srvc_pay_amt for service-only payments or mxd_gd_pay_amt for mixed goods and services.
  • tx_yr, slp_cnt, T1204_TAMT: on the summary, the tax year, the number of slips and the totals for each kind of payment.
  • rpt_tcd: report type. O for original, A for amendment, C for cancel.
